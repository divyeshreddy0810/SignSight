What the forest weighs

After canonicalisation every clip is right-hand-dominant, so the right-hand block carries most of the signal; the left hand matters only for two-handed signs. Static geometry (means) outweighs motion (velocity) — the same story the temporal-resolution ablation told.

Top individual features

    Thumb-relative x offsets dominate: thumb position against the palm separates fists (yes), flat hands (thank you) and pointing shapes (no) better than any other single measurement.
